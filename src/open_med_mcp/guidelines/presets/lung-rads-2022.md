**Source**: American College of Radiology, Lung-RADS Assessment Categories v2022
(https://www.acr.org/Clinical-Resources/Reporting-and-Data-Systems/Lung-Rads). Use only for
screening low-dose CT; for incidental nodules use `fleischner-2017`.

Size = mean of long and short axis diameters to one decimal on lung windows; growth = > 1.5 mm
increase in mean diameter within 12 months.

| category | definition (baseline; see the document for follow-up nuances) | management |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | incomplete: prior CT needed for comparison, or part of the lung not evaluable | comparison / additional imaging |
| 1 | negative: no nodules, or definitely benign nodules (e.g. calcified, fat) | annual screening |
| 2 | benign appearance / behaviour: solid < 6 mm, part-solid < 6 mm, ground-glass < 30 mm, category 3-4 nodules stable >= 3 months, juxtapleural nodules < 10 mm with benign morphology, airway nodules that resolve | annual screening |
| 3 | probably benign: solid 6-< 8 mm at baseline or new 4-< 6 mm; part-solid >= 6 mm with solid component < 6 mm; ground-glass >= 30 mm; atypical pulmonary cyst (growing) | 6-month LDCT |
| 4A | suspicious: solid 8-< 15 mm at baseline, growing < 8 mm, or new 6-< 8 mm; part-solid with solid component 6-< 8 mm or new/growing < 4 mm solid component; endobronchial nodule; thick-walled/multilocular cyst | 3-month LDCT; PET/CT may be used when solid component >= 8 mm |
| 4B | very suspicious: solid >= 15 mm or new/growing >= 8 mm; part-solid with solid component >= 8 mm or new/growing >= 4 mm solid component; slow-growing solid/part-solid nodules over multiple exams; cyst with growing wall/nodularity | diagnostic chest CT with/without contrast, PET/CT and/or tissue sampling |
| 4X | category 3 or 4 nodules with additional features increasing malignancy suspicion (spiculation, lymphadenopathy, growth of GGN) | as 4B |
| S | clinically significant or potentially significant non-lung-cancer finding | as appropriate |

Report the category, the nodule that drives it (size, type, location, growth) and the management
sentence; mention the exam type (baseline vs annual vs interval).
